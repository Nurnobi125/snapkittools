SnapKit Tools Bangla Full Website video kit

Files: script.srt (Bangla subtitles), voiceover-bn.txt (narration), storyboard.md (shot notes), screenshots/ (3 vertical storyboard mockups).

Import the SRT and PNGs into any video editor. The images are designed storyboard mockups, not captured screenshots of the live site. Voiceover is text only; record it or use a Bengali voice in your editor.
