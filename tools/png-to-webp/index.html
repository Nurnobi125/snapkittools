PNG to WebP Converter

Create a WebP copy of a PNG for websites and apps that support modern image formats. Choose a PNG file and download the converted image from your device.

✳ Files are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to SnapKit.

How to convert PNG to WebP

  1. Choose a PNG image from your device.
  2. Select WebP as the output format if it is not already selected.
  3. Convert and download the new image.

WebP output can be smaller, though the result depends on the image and compression. This converter uses the browser's WebP encoder; compare the output before replacing a high-detail original. Transparency is retained by supported browsers.

Smaller images help pages load faster, which is good for visitors and for search. Use this PNG to WebP converter to create a lighter copy for your website, then compare it with the original.

Frequently asked questions

Is this PNG to WebP converter free?

Yes. It is free to use, needs no account, and runs in your browser.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. PNG files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

Why convert PNG to WebP?

WebP files are often smaller than PNG, so pages can load faster. The result depends on the image.

Is WebP supported everywhere?

Current major browsers support WebP, but some older apps and editors may not open it.
