How to merge PDF files

  1. Choose at least two PDF files from your device.
  2. Check their displayed order; the files are merged in that order.
  3. Select Merge PDFs and save the combined document.

Files are merged in the order shown after selection. Large PDFs can use substantial device memory. Password-protected or damaged documents may fail to open.

Use this PDF merger to combine PDF files into one, whether you want to join two PDFs, merge invoices or put scanned pages together. To add pages to an existing PDF, merge it with another PDF that holds the new pages.

Frequently asked questions

Can I merge PDF files for free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Processing happens in this tab. The PDF library is fetched from the CDN on first use, but your selected PDF bytes are not sent to that service.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Some encrypted, damaged, or unusually complex PDFs may not be supported. Use a current browser and a PDF without a password.

How do I combine two PDFs into one?

Select both PDF files, check the order, then merge and download the combined file.

Can I add pages to an existing PDF?

Yes. Merge your existing PDF with a second PDF that has the pages you want to add. Use Extract PDF Pages afterwards if you need to remove some.
