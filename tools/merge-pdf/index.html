Merge PDF Files Online

Join multiple PDF files into a single document without uploading them to a converter server. Choose the files in the order you want them combined, then download the merged PDF.

✳ PDF processing happens on your device. Safe, private, and fast.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Choose at least two PDF files from your device.
  2. Check their displayed order; the files are merged in that order.
  3. Select Merge PDFs and save the combined document.

Files are merged in the order shown after selection. Large PDFs can use substantial device memory. Password-protected or damaged documents may fail to open.

Use this PDF merger to combine PDF files into one, whether you want to join two PDFs, merge invoices or put scanned pages together. To add pages to an existing PDF, merge it with another PDF that holds the new pages. To merge a PDF and a JPG, turn the picture into a PDF with Image to PDF first, then merge both PDFs here.

Frequently asked questions

Can I merge PDF files for free?

Yes. It is free to use, needs no account, and runs entirely in your browser.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Processing happens in this tab. The PDF library is fetched from the CDN on first use, but your selected PDF bytes are not sent to that service.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Some encrypted, damaged, or unusually complex PDFs may not be supported. Use a current browser and a PDF without a password.

How do I combine two PDFs into one?

Select both PDF files, check the order, then merge and download the combined file.

Can I add pages to an existing PDF?

Yes. Merge your existing PDF with a second PDF that has the pages you want to add. Use Extract PDF Pages afterwards if you need to remove some.

How do I merge a PDF and a JPG into one PDF?

Convert the JPG to a PDF with our Image to PDF tool, then select that PDF together with your other PDF here and merge them in the order you want.

Can I merge PDF files online for free without uploading them?

Yes. The files are merged in your browser and are not uploaded to SnapKit. There is no sign-up.

Can I split and merge PDF files online?

Yes. Use Split PDF to break a document into pages or parts, and this tool to merge PDFs again in a new order.
