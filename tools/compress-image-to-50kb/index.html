Compress Image to 50KB Online

Some online forms, exam portals and job applications accept only very small pictures. This page opens the image compressor with a 50 KB limit already filled in, so you can shrink a photo or scan and download a file under 50 KB.

Choose an image ↗

✳ Files are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to SnapKit.

How to compress an image to 50 KB

  1. Choose your photo, signature or scan.
  2. Keep the 50 KB limit (you can change it) and optionally set a maximum width.
  3. Select Compress, check the pixel size shown, and download the file.

50 KB is a tight limit. A passport-style photo or a signature image usually fits, but a large detailed photo may have to be scaled down to a few hundred pixels wide and loses some quality. If the tool cannot reach 50 KB it tells you; try a smaller maximum width.

Use this page when a form says the file must be under 50 KB. The result is the highest quality that still fits the limit, so it usually lands a little below 50 KB rather than exactly on it.

Frequently asked questions

Is this 50 KB image compressor free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and runs in your browser.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read and compressed in your browser and the result is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Very large images, or formats your browser cannot read, may fail. Try a current browser, or convert the image to JPG or PNG first.

How do I make a photo 50 KB or less?

Choose the photo, leave the limit at 50, and select Compress. The tool lowers quality, then the dimensions in small steps, until the file fits.

Will my photo look blurry at 50 KB?

Small files need lower quality or fewer pixels, so some softness is possible. For passport-size photos and signatures it is usually hard to notice at normal size.

Why is my result under 50 KB, not exactly 50?

The tool picks the best quality that is at or below your limit. A result a few KB under the limit is normal.
