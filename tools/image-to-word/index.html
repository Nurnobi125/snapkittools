Image to Word Converter

Turn a photo, scan or screenshot of a page into editable text and save it as a Word (.docx) file.

✳ Images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to SnapKit. The OCR engine downloads from a public CDN on first use.

How to convert an image to Word

  1. Choose a photo or scan of a page.
  2. Pick the text language and select Extract text.
  3. Check the text, then select Download Word (.docx).

The tool reads the text in your image with OCR and saves it as a simple Word document. The Word file contains the recognized text only. Pictures, tables and the original page layout are not recreated.

Need a photo to Word converter? Take a picture of a page, run OCR and download the recognized text as a .docx file you can open in Microsoft Word or Google Docs. The file holds text only, so you will need to restore formatting yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image to Word converter free?

Yes. It is free and needs no account. Your browser does the processing.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read and processed in your browser, not uploaded to SnapKit. The OCR engine (Tesseract.js) and its language data download from a public CDN the first time you use the tool; your image bytes are not sent to it.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

OCR works best on sharp, well-lit images with clear printed text. Blurry photos, handwriting, unusual fonts or low contrast reduce accuracy. Try a higher-resolution image, or crop closer to the text.

Does the Word file keep the layout?

No. It contains the recognized text only. Pictures, tables and the original page layout are not recreated.

Can I open the .docx in Google Docs?

Yes. Word documents open in Microsoft Word, Google Docs and most word processors.
