SVG to PNG Converter

Create a PNG copy of an SVG graphic for apps, forms, or websites that need a raster image. Select an SVG file and download the PNG made by your browser.

Choose SVG file ↗

✳ Files are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to SnapKit.

How to convert SVG to PNG

  1. Choose a SVG image from your device.
  2. Select PNG as the output format if it is not already selected.
  3. Convert and download the new image.

The browser rasterizes the SVG at its intrinsic size. SVG features that rely on external fonts, images, or resources may look different or may not load. Keep the SVG original for scalable editing.

SVG is great for logos and icons, but many sites, apps and documents only accept raster images. This SVG to PNG converter makes a PNG copy you can upload anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is this SVG to PNG converter free?

Yes. It is free to use, needs no account, and runs in your browser.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. SVG files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

Will my PNG be sharp?

PNG is made of pixels, so it is sharp at the size it is created. The converter uses the SVG’s own size, so very small SVGs give small PNGs.

Why does my SVG look different as PNG?

SVGs that depend on external fonts or images may not render exactly the same in a browser.
