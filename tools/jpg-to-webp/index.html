Frequently asked questions

Is this JPG to WebP converter free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. JPG files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

Is WebP better than JPG?

WebP files are often smaller at similar quality, which helps pages load faster. JPG is still more widely supported by older apps.

Will my photo look different?

Usually the difference is hard to see, but results depend on the image and your browser’s encoder. Compare before replacing the original.
