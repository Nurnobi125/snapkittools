Image Compressor – Reduce Image Size Online

Make photos and images smaller for forms, email, websites and messaging apps. Choose one or many images, set the quality or a maximum size in KB, and download the compressed copies.

✳ Files stay on your device. Never uploaded to SnapKit servers.

How to compress an image

  1. Choose one or more images from your device.
  2. Set the quality, or enter a maximum size in KB (for example 100) and optionally a maximum width.
  3. Select Compress, then download each compressed image.

JPG and WebP are compressed with an adjustable quality setting. If you set a maximum size such as 100 KB, the tool lowers quality first and then reduces the dimensions in small steps until the file fits. PNG is lossless, so for a much smaller file choose WebP or JPG; JPG does not keep transparent areas.

Use this image compressor to reduce photo size for online forms, email attachments, websites and chat apps. Pick a quality level, or enter a target such as 50 KB, 100 KB or 200 KB and let the tool find the smallest file that fits. You can compress several pictures at once.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and has no daily limit set by SnapKit. Your device's memory limits very large images.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read and compressed in your browser and the result is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Very large images, or formats your browser cannot read, may fail. Try a current browser, or convert the image to JPG or PNG first. HEIC support depends on your device.

How do I compress an image to 100 KB?

Choose your image, type 100 in the Max size (KB) box, and select Compress. The tool lowers the quality and, if needed, the dimensions until the file is under 100 KB. The same steps work for 50 KB, 200 KB or any other size.

How do I reduce the size of a JPG photo without losing visible quality?

Start with a quality around 70 to 80. Most photos look the same on screen at that level while the file becomes much smaller. If you need a smaller file, lower the quality further or reduce the maximum width.

Can I compress a PNG?

PNG is lossless, so changing quality does not shrink it. For a much smaller file choose WebP or JPG as the output, or set a maximum size. JPG does not keep transparent areas; WebP does.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Yes. Select several images, compress them together, and download each result or use Download all.

How do I reduce a picture from MB to KB?

Set a maximum size in KB, for example 200, and optionally a maximum width such as 1920 px. Large camera photos often shrink from several MB to a few hundred KB with little visible change.
