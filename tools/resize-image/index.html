FREE ONLINE IMAGE TOOL

Resize Image Online Free

Change photo and picture dimensions for social media, websites, forms or email. Enter new dimensions, keep aspect ratio, and download instantly.

✳ Processed locally on your device in real-time. Zero server uploads.

How to resize an image online

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Enter your desired width and height in pixels.
  3. Select Resize & Download to save your image.

Image resizing is performed using browser high-quality canvas bicubic interpolation. Files are processed locally on your device without upload.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. It is free, has no file size limit, and runs completely in your browser.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. Certain image files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.
