FREE ONLINE IMAGE TOOL

Compress Image to 100KB Online

Profile photos, document scans and application forms often ask for images under 100 KB. This page opens the image compressor with a 100 KB limit already filled in, so one click gets you a smaller copy.

✳ Files stay on your device. Never uploaded to SnapKit servers.

How to compress an image to 100 KB

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. Keep the 100 KB limit, or type another size in KB.
  3. Select Compress and download each compressed image.

At 100 KB, a photo around 800 to 1200 pixels wide often still looks good, but it depends on how detailed the picture is. PNG screenshots and scans may be converted to WebP or JPG to reach the limit; JPG does not keep transparent areas.

This is the most common size limit for uploads. If you have several pictures, add them all at once and compress them in one go.

Frequently asked questions

Is this 100 KB image compressor free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and runs in your browser.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read and compressed in your browser and the result is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Very large images, or formats your browser cannot read, may fail. Try a current browser, or convert the image to JPG or PNG first.

How do I compress a JPG to 100 KB?

Choose the JPG, keep the limit at 100 and select Compress. The tool finds the highest quality that fits under 100 KB and reduces the dimensions only if it must.

Can I compress a PNG to 100 KB?

Yes, but PNG is lossless. When you set a size limit, the PNG is saved as WebP (or JPG if WebP is unavailable) so it can reach the target.

Can I compress several images to 100 KB at once?

Yes. Select multiple images; each one is compressed to its own file under the limit.
