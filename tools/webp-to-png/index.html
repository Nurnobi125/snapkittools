How to convert WebP to PNG

  1. Choose a WebP image from your device.
  2. Select PNG as the output format if it is not already selected.
  3. Convert and download the new image.

PNG supports transparent areas, which are preserved when the browser decodes the source correctly. Converting a lossy WebP to PNG does not restore the original image detail, and the resulting PNG may be larger.

Want to edit a WebP graphic or keep its transparent background? Converting WebP to PNG gives you a widely supported, lossless format that works in most editors and design tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is this WebP to PNG converter free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. WebP files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

Does WebP to PNG keep transparency?

Yes. If the WebP image has transparent areas, the PNG keeps them when your browser decodes the file correctly.

Why is the PNG larger than the WebP?

WebP is made for small files, while PNG is lossless. Converting does not restore lost detail and the PNG is often bigger.
