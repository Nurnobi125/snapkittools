Reduce Image Size to 500KB Online

When 100 KB is too small, 500 KB keeps far more detail while still being light enough for websites, blogs and many upload forms. This page opens the compressor with a 500 KB limit already filled in.

✳ Files stay on your device. Never uploaded to SnapKit servers.

How to compress an image to 500 KB

  1. Choose a large photo or scan.
  2. Keep the 500 KB limit, and optionally set a maximum width such as 1920.
  3. Select Compress and download the smaller image.

Large camera photos are often several MB, so reaching 500 KB can mean a drop in dimensions as well as quality. Choose a larger maximum width if you want to keep the picture bigger and accept a lower quality instead.

Use this page for website images, blog photos and scans that must stay readable but are too heavy to upload.

Frequently asked questions

Is this 500 KB image compressor free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and runs in your browser.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read and compressed in your browser and the result is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Very large images, or formats your browser cannot read, may fail. Try a current browser, or convert the image to JPG or PNG first.

How do I reduce a JPG to under 500 KB?

Select the JPG, keep the limit at 500 and compress. A multi-MB photo usually reaches 500 KB with little visible change.

How do I reduce a picture from MB to KB?

Set the size you need in KB, for example 500, and compress. 1 MB is about 1,024 KB.
