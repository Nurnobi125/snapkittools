How to convert SVG to JPG

  1. Choose a SVG image from your device.
  2. Select JPG as the output format if it is not already selected.
  3. Convert and download the new image.

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent parts are filled white. The SVG is rasterized at the dimensions provided by the file and JPG compression may soften fine details.

Frequently asked questions

Is this SVG to JPG converter free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. SVG files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

What happens to a transparent SVG?

JPG has no transparency, so transparent parts are filled with white.

Should I choose JPG or PNG?

Choose JPG for smaller files, and PNG when you need sharp edges. Use SVG to PNG if transparency matters.
