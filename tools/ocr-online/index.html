Free Online OCR

Upload a photo, scan or screenshot and turn the printed text into editable text. Copy it, or download it as a text or Word file.

✳ Images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to SnapKit. The OCR engine downloads from a public CDN on first use.

How to use OCR online

  1. Choose one or more images from your device.
  2. Pick the text language, English, Bangla or both.
  3. Select Extract text, check the result, then copy or download it.

OCR (optical character recognition) reads letters in a picture and turns them into text you can edit. Accuracy depends on the image: clear printed text works best, while handwriting and blurry photos work poorly.

Use this free OCR online tool to copy text out of a picture instead of typing it again. It works with photos of documents, screenshots, signs and scanned pages, and you can save the result as a text file or a Word document.

Frequently asked questions

Is this online OCR tool free?

Yes. It is free and needs no account. Your browser does the processing.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read and processed in your browser, not uploaded to SnapKit. The OCR engine (Tesseract.js) and its language data download from a public CDN the first time you use the tool; your image bytes are not sent to it.

What if the result doesn't look right?

OCR works best on sharp, well-lit images with clear printed text. Blurry photos, handwriting, unusual fonts or low contrast reduce accuracy. Try a higher-resolution image, or crop closer to the text.

What is OCR?

OCR stands for optical character recognition. It finds letters in an image and converts them to text you can edit and search.

Why is some of my text wrong?

OCR can misread blurry, small or decorative text, and handwriting is usually not recognized well. Use a sharp, high-resolution image and proofread the result.
