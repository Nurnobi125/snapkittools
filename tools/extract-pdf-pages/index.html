Extract Pages from a PDF

Create a smaller PDF containing only the pages you need. Enter page numbers or ranges such as 1-3,5, then download the extracted pages as a new PDF.

✳ Files are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to SnapKit.

How to extract PDF pages

  1. Choose one PDF from your device.
  2. Enter the pages to keep, for example 1-3,5.
  3. Select Extract Pages and download the new PDF.

Page numbers start at 1. For example, 1-3,5 keeps pages 1, 2, 3, and 5, in that order. The original PDF remains unchanged on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract PDF pages without uploading the file?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Processing happens in this tab. The PDF library is fetched from the CDN on first use, but your selected PDF bytes are not sent to that service.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Some encrypted, damaged, or unusually complex PDFs may not be supported. Use a current browser and a PDF without a password.
