How to convert JPG to PNG

  1. Choose a JPG image from your device.
  2. Select PNG as the output format if it is not already selected.
  3. Convert and download the new image.

PNG output does not restore quality lost in the original JPG. The new file may be larger, especially for photographs. Use the JPG original when smaller file size matters.

Looking for a quick JPEG to PNG converter? This page lets you convert JPG to PNG free, without installing software. It works for a single photo or a screenshot saved as JPG, and the PNG is created on your own device.

Frequently asked questions

Is this JPG to PNG converter free?

Yes. It is free to use, needs no account, and runs in your browser.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. JPG files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

Does JPG to PNG make the background transparent?

No. JPG has no transparency, so the new PNG keeps the same background. To remove a background you need a separate background remover.

Why is my PNG bigger than the JPG?

PNG is lossless, so photos usually become larger when saved as PNG. Keep the JPG if file size matters.
