How to convert AVIF to JPG

  1. Choose a AVIF image from your device.
  2. Select JPG as the output format if it is not already selected.
  3. Convert and download the new image.

AVIF support varies by browser and device. If the image does not open, try a browser with AVIF support or convert it with an image app that can read AVIF. JPG does not preserve transparency.

AVIF gives small files, but many apps cannot open it yet. Use this AVIF to JPEG converter to get a universal JPG that works in email, documents and image editors.

Frequently asked questions

Is this AVIF to JPG converter free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. AVIF files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

What is an AVIF file?

AVIF is a newer image format that gives small files at good quality. Support is still growing across apps and devices.

Why does my AVIF not convert?

Your browser must be able to open AVIF first. Try an up-to-date browser if the file does not load.
