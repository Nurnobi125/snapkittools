Frequently asked questions

Is this essay word counter free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Is my input uploaded?

No. This tool runs in your browser, and the text or values you enter are not sent to SnapKit.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

This free browser tool runs locally in the current tab. No account or paid API is required. Results can depend on the text you paste and browser support.

How many words is a typical essay?

It depends on the assignment. Always follow your teacher’s or exam’s word limit. Short school essays are often a few hundred words, while longer papers can be much longer.

Does the count include the title and references?

It counts whatever text you paste. Remove parts that your limit does not include, then check the count again.
