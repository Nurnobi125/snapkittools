Bangla OCR Online

Read Bangla text from a photo, scan or screenshot and turn it into editable text. Bangla is selected by default; you can also read English together with Bangla.

✳ Images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to SnapKit. The OCR engine downloads from a public CDN on first use.

How to extract Bangla text from an image

  1. Choose an image with printed Bangla text.
  2. Keep the language set to Bangla, or choose English + Bangla for mixed text.
  3. Select Extract text, then proofread and copy or download the result.

Bangla OCR is harder than English OCR, so results are less accurate, especially for small print, old books and handwriting. Use a sharp, high-resolution image of printed text and proofread the output.

This Bangla OCR (Bengali OCR) tool reads printed Bangla from images and turns it into text you can copy. Accuracy is lower than for English, so use a clear, high-resolution image and proofread the output.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Bangla OCR tool free?

Yes. It is free and needs no account. Your browser does the processing.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read and processed in your browser, not uploaded to SnapKit. The OCR engine (Tesseract.js) and its language data download from a public CDN the first time you use the tool; your image bytes are not sent to it.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

OCR works best on sharp, well-lit images with clear printed text. Blurry photos, handwriting, unusual fonts or low contrast reduce accuracy. Try a higher-resolution image, or crop closer to the text.

How accurate is Bangla OCR?

It is less accurate than English OCR. Clean printed text works best, while small print, old books and handwriting often have mistakes.

Can it read Bangla and English together?

Yes. Choose English + Bangla for pages with both languages.
