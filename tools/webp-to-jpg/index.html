How to convert WebP to JPG

  1. Choose a WebP image from your device.
  2. Select JPG as the output format if it is not already selected.
  3. Convert and download the new image.

JPG works in a wide range of apps and devices. It does not keep transparent pixels, so transparent areas are filled with white. JPG uses lossy compression; converting cannot recover detail that was already lost in the WebP source.

Saved a picture from a website and it will not open? Many sites serve WebP images that older apps and editors cannot read. Use this WebP to JPEG converter to get a file that opens almost anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is this WebP to JPG converter free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. WebP files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

Why can’t I open my WebP image?

Some older programs and apps do not support WebP. Converting it to JPG makes it open on nearly every device.

Does WebP to JPG keep transparency?

No. JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white. Use WebP to PNG if you need to keep transparency.
