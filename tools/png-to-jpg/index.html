How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Choose a PNG image from your device.
  2. Select JPG as the output format if it is not already selected.
  3. Convert and download the new image.

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent pixels are flattened onto a white background. JPG is lossy, so keep the PNG original if you need lossless editing or crisp text and line art.

Need a smaller image for a website, form or email? Use this PNG to JPEG converter to change PNG to JPG in seconds. Because JPG files are usually lighter, they are easier to upload and share.

Frequently asked questions

Is this PNG to JPG converter free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Do you upload or store my image?

No. The image is read locally by your browser and the converted copy is downloaded to your device. GitHub Pages may process normal website request logs as the host.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Image decoding depends on your browser. PNG files may not be supported on every device. Try a current browser or use a local image app that supports the file.

What happens to transparent areas in a PNG?

JPG cannot store transparency, so transparent pixels are placed on a white background.

Will I lose quality when I convert PNG to JPG?

JPG is lossy, so a small amount of detail can be lost. Keep your PNG original for editing or for sharp text and line art.
