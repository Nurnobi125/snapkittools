Rotate PDF Pages Online

Fix a PDF whose pages display sideways or upside down. Choose a PDF, select the rotation angle, and download a rotated copy.

Choose a PDF to rotate ↗

✳ Files are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to SnapKit.

How to rotate PDF pages

  1. Choose one PDF from your device.
  2. Select 90, 180, or 270 degrees.
  3. Select Rotate PDF and save the new file.

The selected angle is applied to every page and added to each page's existing rotation. The original file is not changed.

Fix a scanned PDF that opens sideways or upside down. Choose 90, 180 or 270 degrees and save a corrected copy. The rotation is applied to every page of the document.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate a PDF for free without uploading it?

Yes. It is free to use, needs no account, and runs in your browser.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Processing happens in this tab. The PDF library is fetched from the CDN on first use, but your selected PDF bytes are not sent to that service.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Some encrypted, damaged, or unusually complex PDFs may not be supported. Use a current browser and a PDF without a password.

Can I rotate only some pages?

This tool rotates all pages by the angle you pick. To rotate only some pages, extract them first with Extract PDF Pages.

Is the rotation saved in the new file?

Yes. The downloaded copy keeps the new orientation, and your original file is not changed.
