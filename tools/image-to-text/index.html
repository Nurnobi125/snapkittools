Image to Text Converter

Turn a picture of text into editable text. Choose an image, run OCR and copy the result, or download it as a text or Word file.

✳ Images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to SnapKit. The OCR engine downloads from a public CDN on first use.

How to convert an image to text

  1. Choose an image that contains text.
  2. Select the language of the text.
  3. Select Extract text and copy or download the result.

This converter uses OCR to read printed text from photos, scans and screenshots. Review the output before you use it, because OCR can misread similar letters, small print and decorative fonts.

Looking for a picture to text converter? Choose an image with printed text and the tool turns it into text you can copy, edit or save. It is handy for screenshots, scanned notes, receipts and book pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image to text converter free?

Yes. It is free and needs no account. Your browser does the processing.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read and processed in your browser, not uploaded to SnapKit. The OCR engine (Tesseract.js) and its language data download from a public CDN the first time you use the tool; your image bytes are not sent to it.

What if the result doesn't look right?

OCR works best on sharp, well-lit images with clear printed text. Blurry photos, handwriting, unusual fonts or low contrast reduce accuracy. Try a higher-resolution image, or crop closer to the text.

Which images work best?

Sharp, well-lit images with dark text on a plain background work best. Crop away extra background before you run the tool.

Can it read handwriting?

Not reliably. The tool is made for printed text, and handwriting often comes out wrong.
