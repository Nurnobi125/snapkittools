PRIVACY

Your files stay on your device.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

In this static version of SnapKit, image conversion, resizing, and color analysis happen in your device's browser. Images are not uploaded to a SnapKit application server. Everyday text, developer, math/date, and unit tools also run in your browser; your input is not sent to SnapKit.

Files and downloads

An image you select opens in your browser's local memory. Your browser creates a new file on your device for download. Temporary working data is cleared when you close the page.

Website hosting

If you publish this site on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process browser requests and security-related logs. GitHub's own privacy policy and terms apply. The first time you use a PDF tool, the required PDF library loads in your browser from the jsDelivr CDN: pdf-lib for editing pages, and PDF.js for rendering a page to PNG or JPG. Your selected PDF bytes are not sent to the CDN. Standard request information related to loading the library may be visible to the CDN. This version has no SnapKit analytics, ads, cookies, accounts, or contact form.

Chrome extension

The SnapKit Tools Chrome extension stores only the tools you choose as favorites, up to eight recently opened tool shortcuts, and your light or dark theme preference in the browser local storage for the extension. This information stays on your device, is not sent to SnapKit or shared with third parties, and is removed when you remove the extension or clear its local data. The extension does not read your browsing history or the contents of pages you visit.

Limitations

Opening and converting some images depends on your browser's format support and your device's memory. The EXIF cleaner creates a new PNG copy but does not guarantee removal of every kind of metadata. Check the result before using sensitive files.

Policy changes

If analytics, advertising, accounts, or server-based features are added in the future, this page should be updated to explain what information is collected and why.
