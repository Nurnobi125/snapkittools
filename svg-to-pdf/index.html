How to convert SVG to PDF

  1. Choose an SVG graphic from your device.
  2. Pick a page size, orientation, SVG render resolution, fit mode, and margin.
  3. Select Create PDF and save the generated document.

The SVG is rasterized by your browser at the selected 1×–4× resolution and placed on the selected PDF page. Higher resolution can improve print sharpness, increase memory use, and still does not create editable vector paths. This produces a printable PDF, not an editable vector PDF. External SVG resources may not render.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert an SVG file to PDF for free?

Yes. This static version has no account or paid API. Your browser does the processing.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are read locally by your browser, and the PDF library is fetched from the CDN on first use. Your selected image bytes are not sent to the CDN.

Why can't my file open or tool run?

Some encrypted, damaged, or unusually complex PDFs may not be supported. Use a current browser and a PDF without a password.
