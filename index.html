Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

Images open and process in your browser in this version. Files are not sent to SnapKit servers.

Why will some images not open?

Image support depends on your browser. HEIC/HEIF in particular does not work in every browser. Try converting the file to JPEG or PNG first.

Does the EXIF cleaner remove all metadata?

It creates a new PNG copy that usually has no EXIF data. It does not guarantee removal of every kind of metadata; check sensitive images before sharing.
